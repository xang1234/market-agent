## 3. Watch items

### a. Judge leniency: 15 entailed inferences checked by hand

From the 55 inferences today's judge rated entailed and not worded, 15 were drawn at random (seed 15). Each is checked against its supports and the figures on the page (`2026-10-23-leniency-sample.txt`). I would back 13 of the 15.

I would not back these two:

1. `cause-in-evidence #0 [8]`: "The positive net income despite an operating loss cuts against reading the quarter as a broad earnings deterioration, since the packet does not explain the offset." Net income of $872.0M against an operating loss of -$134.0M only says something below the operating line offset the loss. Nothing shown says that offset is recurring or that earnings did not deteriorate. The judge gave no reason. The wording also leaks "the packet", an internal term.
2. `fiscal-year-trap #1 [4]`: "It has the larger revenue base, but its margins are well below the other company's, so the scale does not carry through to profitability." The margins are right (AAPL 26.9% net against NVDA 55.8%). But AAPL's net income, $416.2B x 26.9% = about $112B, is larger than NVDA's $130.5B x 55.8% = about $73B. So scale does carry through to profit dollars. The judge read "profitability" as margins only.

One inconsistency, not a wrong verdict. `ticker-page-compare #0 [6]`, "Its far stronger fundamentals did not translate into a clearly better share price return", was rated entailed. Two near-identical inferences in samples #1 and #2 of the same trace were rated unsupported ("the answer never links fundamentals to share price"). The same claim lands on different tiers from run to run.

The 2026-10-18 example, "driven by a one-time charge" with no source calling it one-time, did not appear. No answer today contains "one-time", "one-off", "temporary", "transitory" or "non-recurring".

Seven of the 15 entailed verdicts carry no reason text. The judge prompt asks for none on an entailed verdict that is not worded (`inference-judge.ts`, "Give a reason for every verdict but entailed"), so a reader of the trace cannot see why one passed. The first of the two I would not back is among the seven.

Why proposed citations drop out. This is a guess, partly measured. Two paths account for what I could see. Second, accepted `unknown_cause` assertions and analyst inferences cite the series as evidence, but neither renders a figure ref. The rest is not traced.

The lab traces themselves have no derived figures (section 1). The latest-quarter QoQ metric, which predates #478, was cited in 10 of 42 lab answers today against 5 of 28 on 2026-10-18.

## 4. Engine-route commentary (#486)

**Commentary is not reachable from a live question on main.** It is written only on a company overview, which needs the engine lane, a company, and a scope reading that asks for no figure (`company-overview.ts` `asksForNoFigure`). The engine lane takes only a question with a metric word (`financial-runtime.ts` `FINANCIAL_TERMS`) until #398 step 6 routes "Analyze NVDA" there. I asked 11 live questions, and none produced commentary.

- "Tell me about NVDA", "How is AMD doing financially?", "How does NVDA compare with its peers?" and "Give me an overview of AAPL" went to the narrative lane.
- "Analyze NVDA's profit", "Compare NVDA and AMD profit" and five more phrasings with a metric word reached the engine lane. All seven came back as a clarifying question, such as which profit measure, which peers, or which EPS figure and period. The menu comes from the planner prompt's ambiguity rule (`financial-engine/src/planner.ts:465`, since 2026-09-24). The same two questions gave the same menu on 4e1f7c0 and 549b74b, so it is not a regression from #502 or #504. #517 asks for an answer with stated defaults instead.
