Offline and dev only (#610). Inputs are the engine's selection as of the 10-K filing date + 1 day, with each SEC fact taken as public by the end of its filing date in New York. The price is the first close on or after that day, a yfinance split-adjusted close, never certified. Required growth is the growth of total free cash flow that price requires at its multiple, with ADR 0006's model, the 10% required return and d, the share change stock-based compensation implies. Trailing growth is the same total free cash flow's growth to this 10-K from the ones 3 and 5 fiscal years earlier, each read as of its own filing date + 1 day, so known at the as-of date. Realized growth is its growth to the 10-K five fiscal years later; the realized share change over those years, buybacks included, sits beside d. Each growth is also shown per share, the earlier count restated for splits. The price return is annualized over five years from that close, price only, with no dividends.

Against the 2026-11-21 run, every row free cash flow blocked on `operating_cash_flow context_unverified` now has a free cash flow. - **Realized growth.** None of the 24 gains it. 22 read `incompatible_definition`, because the row's year is continuing-operations OCF and FY+5 is the total, the rule #741 set for capex. HON FY2016 and MRK FY2021 have no FY+5 facts. - **Trailing growth.** 18 rows gain a 3-year trailing rate and 8 a 5-year one, where both ends are in one scope. A window across the switch reads `incompatible_definition`.

So the rows reaching both figures stay at 76. Whether a window across a switch to the total should count as one measure is the operator's call. CAT's FY2019 10-K, for one, restates FY2018 OCF as a total equal to the continuing-operations figure.

## How it was run

The runbook is the header of `services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`. Everything in this doc except the Answer, How it was run and Caveats sections is the script's output.

- The stack was this checkout's own Compose project at port offset 455. Only its Postgres ran, migrated and seeded.
- The weekly reference import loaded 10,054 listings from the GitHub release bundle dated 2026-10-05.
- **No SEC requests.** Every SEC body came from a copy of the 2026-11-21 run's cache (`.dev/sec-cache`, 25 companyfacts bodies and `company_tickers.json`, hashes checked against the original) through that run's fetch guard at a cap of 0. Each process logged 0 network requests, and the guard's request log kept the 26 lines it had. `fill:ciks` filled 5,936 issuers, as on 2026-11-21. The submissions endpoint was refused, as then.
- Splits came from Polygon `sync:splits` for the 25 listings, retried one at a time after HTTP 429s.
- Closes came from yfinance through the dev-providers sidecar (`capture:closes` on 2026-11-22). The captures were then cut to 2016-11-21 to 2026-11-20, the 2026-11-21 run's window, so prices match it.
- The same data was backfilled and backtested twice, first with origin/main (81b95125) and `backfill:sec-statements --annual-history-years 16`, then with this branch's backfill over the stored facts. That gave the 65 continuing-operations OCF originals main had left without a context their scope and a context, and stored 15 later 10-Ks' restatements of them, scoped too. OCF facts with a context went from 369 of 434 to 449 of 449. Costco's backfill stopped on the same `eps_basic` unit error as on 2026-11-21, and JNJ's FY2024 balance sheet on the same `CommercialPaper` disagreement.
- **Scope marks.** Each free cash flow cell names the scopes it was computed in, as "Free cash flow (continuing-operations OCF less PP&E cash capex)". The doc was regenerated for them on a fresh stack at offset 490, from another hash-checked copy of the same cache, with this branch's backfill alone. Its rows equal the first run's once the marks are removed, and the request log stayed 26 lines.
- **Reproduction.** main's run matches the 2026-11-21 doc row for row except CAT, HD, MRK, PEP and QCOM, whose productive-assets capex #741 certified. This branch's run differs from main's only in rows that named `operating_cash_flow context_unverified`.

## Caveats

- **Publication rule, prices, ties and survivorship** are as in the 2026-11-21 run's caveats, which hold here unchanged.
- **Scope at each end.** A row reads each 10-K as first filed, so a year filed for continuing operations keeps that scope even where a later 10-K restates it as an equal total.
