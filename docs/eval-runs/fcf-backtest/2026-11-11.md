# FCF required-growth backtest, 2026-11-11

A dev-only run of `npm run backtest:fcf` (#610) over 25 large US issuers. Each row reads the Call's inputs as of the day after the FY 10-K's filing date. It solves the growth that day's close required at that day's multiple (ADR 0006, 10% required return). Beside that it sets what followed: the FY free cash flow per share CAGR to the 10-K five fiscal years later, and the five-year price return against the 10% hurdle. Nothing here is certified, and nothing was written to the DB by the backtest.

## How it was run

The runbook is the header of `services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`. On this run:

- `backfill:sec-statements --annual-history-years 11` ran for the 25 issuers with attached CIKs. It made 25 SEC requests, one `data.sec.gov` companyfacts download per issuer, and no others. Each body is cached locally, so a rerun makes no request. All 25 issuers backfilled 11 periods.
- The SEC submissions endpoint was not called. The backfill logs its acceptance-metadata fetch as failed and continues, so no fact carries an acceptance time.
- One statement extraction failed: JNJ's FY2024 balance sheet (`CommercialPaper` period disagreement). FY2024 is outside the 2016 to 2021 sample, so no row reads it as a base year.
- Then `npm run backtest:fcf -- --tickers CAT,COST,MSFT,KO,PEP,PG,JNJ,WMT,HD,MCD,NKE,UNP,TXN,CSCO,INTC,ORCL,MMM,HON,LOW,SBUX,ABT,TMO,AMGN,QCOM,MRK`.

## Summary

- **Rows.** 145 issuer-years. 70 solved a required growth. 61 have both a required growth and a realized FCF-per-share CAGR.
- **Realized against required.** In 29 of the 61, realized FCF-per-share growth was at or above the required growth. The median gap, realized minus required, is -1.0 percentage points.
- **Against the hurdle.** 60 of the 61 also have a 5-year price return.
  - Of the 29 that beat required growth, 18 returned at or above 10% a year. Their median was 12.1% a year.
  - Of the 31 priced rows that did not, 5 returned at or above 10% a year. Their median was 6.1% a year.
- **Why 75 rows solved no required growth**, by the first outcome the required-growth cell names.
  - 48 `blocked_by_dependency`. Operating cash flow or capex had no certified context (`context_unverified`).
  - 16 `missing_input` for net cash.
  - 7 `missing_input` for stock-based compensation.
  - 3 `missing_input` for shares (NKE).
  - 1 `price_missing` (PG FY2016, filed 2016-08-09, before captures start on 2016-11-10).
- **Why 9 of the 70 have no realized growth.** All 9 are FY2021 rows with `missing_input`, because the issuer has not yet filed its FY2026 10-K.

The `blocked_by_dependency` rows follow the filers' tags. A capex fact certifies only from `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, and operating cash flow only from `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities` (`SEC_CERTIFIED_CONCEPTS`, `services/fundamentals/src/sec-facts-repository.ts`). QCOM, PEP and HD tag `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` in every sample year. MRK does through FY2022, and CAT through FY2019. CAT, COST, CSCO, HON, INTC, LOW, MMM, MSFT, ORCL, TXN and UNP lack the certified OCF concept in their earlier 10-Ks.

## Caveats

- **Publication rule.** A fact counts as public by the end of its filing's SEC filing date in New York. The backtest applies this bound in memory, beside each fact's own proofs. It is never stored and never certifies anything (`services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`, header). Without it, the engine has no inputs at a 2017 cutoff.
- **Prices.** Closes are yfinance split-adjusted `daily_close_captures` on the dev DB, never certified. The price return is price only, with no dividends.
- **AAPL and GOOGL are skipped.** #597 keeps their listings' issuers apart from the SEC issuers.
- **Late FY2021 rows have no 5-year price.** The latest captured close is 2026-11-09, so every row filed after 2021-11-09 reads `stale_price`. That is 16 FY2021 rows and HON's FY2020 row (see below). It leaves 10 of the 70 rows with a required growth unpriced.
- **Small N and survivorship.** 61 rows from 25 issuers, and every issuer is a large company that exists today.
- **Filer tags mislabel three rows.** The ingest takes a statement's fiscal year from the filer's companyfacts `fy`.
  - HON tagged its FY2021 10-K (filed 2022-02-11) as fiscal 2020. Its FY2020 row therefore holds FY2021 statements as of 2022-02-12, and HON has no FY2021 row. That row is in the 61. Without it, 29 of 60 beat, and the median gap is -0.6 points. The hurdle split is unchanged, since the row has no price.
  - NKE reports no `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` cover count. Its statements then carry no filing date. Its FY2016 row takes the filing date of the FY2018 10-K that restated FY2016, and NKE has no FY2019 to FY2021 rows. No NKE row solves a required growth.
  - COST tagged its FY2019 10-K as `fp` Q4, so COST has no FY2019 row.
- **Outliers stay in.** SBUX FY2020 shows a 1,084.72x multiple on depressed FY2020 free cash flow, and a realized CAGR of 85.7%. The medians above include it.
