## How it was run

The runbook is the header of `services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`. Everything in this doc except this section and the caveats is the script's output. This section and the caveats come from `docs/eval-runs/fcf-backtest/2026-11-11.notes.md` through `--notes`. From `services/fundamentals`, the command above writes the doc with `> ../../docs/eval-runs/fcf-backtest/2026-11-11.md`.

- `backfill:sec-statements --annual-history-years 11` ran for the 25 issuers with attached CIKs. It made 25 SEC requests, one `data.sec.gov` companyfacts download per issuer, and no others. Each body is cached locally, so a rerun makes no request. All 25 issuers backfilled 11 periods.
- The SEC submissions endpoint was not called. The backfill logs its acceptance-metadata fetch as failed and continues, so no fact carries an acceptance time.
- One statement extraction failed: JNJ's FY2024 balance sheet (`CommercialPaper` period disagreement). FY2024 is outside the 2016 to 2021 sample, so no row reads it as a base year.
- The backtest reads only the dev DB. This doc was regenerated after the round 1 review fixes with the SEC fetch guard preloaded, and it counted 0 SEC requests.

The `blocked_by_dependency` rows follow the filers' tags. A capex fact certifies only from `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, and operating cash flow only from `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities` (`SEC_CERTIFIED_CONCEPTS`, `services/fundamentals/src/sec-facts-repository.ts`). QCOM, PEP and HD tag `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` in every sample year. MRK does through FY2022, and CAT through FY2019. CAT, COST, CSCO, HON, INTC, LOW, MMM, MSFT, ORCL, TXN and UNP lack the certified OCF concept in their earlier 10-Ks.

## Caveats

- **Publication rule.** A fact counts as public by the end of its filing's SEC filing date in New York. The backtest applies this bound in memory, beside each fact's own proofs. It is never stored and never certifies anything (`services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`, header). Without it, the engine has no inputs at a 2017 cutoff.
- **Prices.** Closes are yfinance split-adjusted `daily_close_captures` on the dev DB, never certified. The price return is price only, with no dividends.
- **AAPL and GOOGL are skipped.** #597 keeps their listings' issuers apart from the SEC issuers.
- **Late FY2021 rows have no 5-year price.** The latest captured close is 2026-11-09, so every row filed after 2021-11-09 reads `stale_price`. That is 16 FY2021 rows and HON's FY2020 row. It leaves 10 of the 70 rows with a required growth unpriced.
- **Ties.** Required growth is the solver's bisection midpoint, within 0.005 points. Realized against required is compared unrounded, and a tie counts as at or above. ABT FY2019 is the one row inside the solver's precision: 7.4997% required against 7.5005% realized. It counts as at or above.
- **Small N and survivorship.** 60 rows from 25 issuers, and every issuer is a large company that exists today.
- **Filer tags mislabel rows.** The ingest takes a statement's fiscal year from the filer's companyfacts `fy`. The summary leaves out every `period_mismatch` row.
  - HON tagged its FY2021 10-K (filed 2022-02-11) as fiscal 2020. Its FY2020 row holds FY2021 statements as of 2022-02-12, so it is a `period_mismatch`. HON's FY2021 row is `no_fy_facts`.
  - NKE reports no `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` cover count, so its statements mostly carry no filing date. Its FY2016 row takes the filing date of the FY2018 10-K that restated FY2016 and reads FY2018 statements, so it is a `period_mismatch`. FY2019 to FY2021 are `no_filing_date`. No NKE row solves a required growth.
  - COST tagged its FY2019 10-K as `fp` Q4, so its FY2019 row is `no_fy_facts`.
- **Outliers stay in.** SBUX FY2020 shows a 1,084.72x multiple on depressed FY2020 free cash flow, and a realized CAGR of 85.68%. The medians above include it.
