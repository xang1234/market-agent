## How it was run

The runbook is the header of `services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`. Everything in this doc except this section and the caveats is the script's output. This section and the caveats come from `docs/eval-runs/fcf-backtest/2026-11-11.notes.md` through `--notes`. From `services/fundamentals`, the command above writes the doc with `> ../../docs/eval-runs/fcf-backtest/2026-11-11.md`.

- `backfill:sec-statements --annual-history-years 11` ran for the 25 issuers with attached CIKs. It made 25 SEC requests, one `data.sec.gov` companyfacts download per issuer, and no others. Each body is cached locally, so a rerun makes no request. All 25 issuers backfilled 11 periods.
- The SEC submissions endpoint was not called. The backfill logs its acceptance-metadata fetch as failed and continues, so no fact carries an acceptance time.
- One statement extraction failed: JNJ's FY2024 balance sheet (`CommercialPaper` period disagreement). FY2024 is outside the 2016 to 2021 sample, so no row reads it as a base year.
- The backtest reads only the dev DB. This doc was regenerated after the round 3 review fixes with the SEC fetch guard preloaded at a fetch cap of 0 and no SEC user agent set, and it counted 0 SEC requests.

The `blocked_by_dependency` rows follow the filers' tags. A capex fact certifies only from `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, and operating cash flow only from `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities` (`SEC_CERTIFIED_CONCEPTS`, `services/fundamentals/src/sec-facts-repository.ts`). QCOM, PEP and HD tag `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` in every sample year. MRK does through FY2022, and CAT through FY2019. CAT, COST, CSCO, HON, INTC, LOW, MMM, MSFT, ORCL, TXN and UNP lack the certified OCF concept in their earlier 10-Ks.

## Caveats

- **Publication rule.** A fact counts as public by the end of its filing's SEC filing date in New York. The backtest applies this bound in memory, beside each fact's own proofs. It is never stored and never certifies anything (`services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`, header). Without it, the engine has no inputs at a 2017 cutoff.
- **Prices.** Closes are yfinance split-adjusted `daily_close_captures` on the dev DB, never certified. Each row is priced at the first close on or after its as-of day, at most 4 days later (`MAX_CLOSE_AGE_DAYS`, `services/fundamentals/src/fcf-backtest.ts`), so a 10-K filed before a weekend or holiday is priced after it, never at the filing day's own close. 45 of the 150 rows are priced after their as-of day, 38 by 2 days and 7 by 3. WMT filed FY2018 on Good Friday 2018-03-30 and is priced at 2018-04-02. A longer wait is a gap in the captures, not a market closure, so the row reads `stale_price`. The dev captures start at 2016-11-10, so 7 FY2016 rows read `stale_price`: COST, MSFT, PG, WMT, CSCO, ORCL and QCOM (as of Thursday 2016-11-03).
- **The hurdle is price only.** The 5-year return set against the 10% hurdle is the change in the adjusted close, with no dividends. A name that pays out more of its cash as dividends shows a lower return than its holders earned, so the hurdle split is biased against high-yield names.
- **Realized growth is total free cash flow.** Required growth is the growth of total free cash flow, with only the share change `d` that stock-based compensation implies (ADR 0006 leaves buybacks out). So realized growth is total free cash flow too, and the realized share change, buybacks included, sits beside `d`. Across the 60 compared rows the median `d` is 0.22% a year and the median realized share change is about -1.1% a year, and 52 of the 60 counts shrank. LOW's FY2017 row grew free cash flow 11.39% a year while its count fell 6.30% a year.
- **AAPL and GOOGL are skipped.** #597 keeps their listings' issuers apart from the SEC issuers.
- **Late FY2021 rows have no 5-year price.** The return ends at the first close on or after five years from the row's first close. The latest captured close is 2026-11-09, so a row has a 5-year price only when its first close is on or before 2021-11-09, which takes a 10-K filed on or before 2021-11-08. Every later row reads `price_missing`. That is 16 FY2021 rows and HON's FY2020 row (QCOM FY2021, filed 2021-11-03, is the last priced; SBUX FY2021, filed 2021-11-19, the first not). It leaves 10 of the 70 rows with a required growth unpriced.
- **Ties.** Required growth is the solver's bisection midpoint, within 0.005 points. Realized against required is compared unrounded, and a tie counts as at or above. No row in this run is inside the solver's precision. Required growth prints at the model's one decimal, so LOW FY2018 reads 4.2% required against 4.24% realized, yet it is below: 4.2496% against 4.2361%, 0.0135 points apart.
- **Small N and survivorship.** 60 rows from 15 of the 25 issuers (CAT, PEP, WMT, HD, NKE, INTC, ORCL, MMM, QCOM and MRK contribute none), and every issuer is a large company that exists today.
- **Filer tags mislabel rows.** The ingest takes a statement's fiscal year from the filer's companyfacts `fy`. The summary leaves out every `period_mismatch` row.
  - HON tagged its FY2021 10-K (filed 2022-02-11) as fiscal 2020. Its FY2020 row holds FY2021 statements as of 2022-02-12, so it is a `period_mismatch`. HON's FY2021 row is `no_fy_facts`.
  - NKE reports no `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` cover count, so its statements mostly carry no filing date. Its FY2016 row takes the filing date of the FY2018 10-K that restated FY2016 and reads FY2018 statements, so it is a `period_mismatch`. FY2019 to FY2021 are `no_filing_date`. No NKE row solves a required growth.
  - COST tagged its FY2019 10-K as `fp` Q4, so its FY2019 row is `no_fy_facts`.
- **Outliers stay in.** SBUX FY2020 shows a 1,084.72x multiple on depressed FY2020 free cash flow, and a realized CAGR of 84.51%. The medians above include it.
