## Decision

No prompt rule is kept. Each candidate fixed its own case and broke another.

- Candidate A ("an outlook is guidance") fixed #31 and broke #30, its mirror.
- Candidate B ("a margin turning negative in a quarter is its trend") fixed
  #57 and broke #49.

The prompt is unchanged.

The owner decided #31 on 2026-11-11. A why-question about beating the outlook
asks for the cause, not for the guidance itself, so #31 now expects
`guidance: false`, as #29 and #30 do. Its case carries a `note` saying so.
Only #31's entry in `test/fixtures/scope-eval-replies.json` was replaced, with
one live reply that resolves to the new expectation. The rest of the file was
not re-recorded, because #49 and #57 drift live. Under the new expectation
`--cases=31 --runs=10` misses 0 of 10.

#57 keeps its pinned route `trend`, and its case `note` records the pin
(#504, `edceaf63`, pending #511).

## #31: the expectation contradicts its neighbours

"Why was NVDA's gross margin above the high end of its outlook?" expects
`guidance: true`. Its neighbours expect `guidance: false`:

- #29 "Why did NVDA beat its guidance?"
- #30 "Why did NVDA's revenue come in below the low end of guidance?"

The prompt's rule says the same: "A result set against expectations asks why,
not for guidance ... 'beat guidance' ask explains". #31 was first recorded
with `guidance: false` (`b2bb770b`). The re-record in `edceaf63` (#504)
flipped it to `true`, and that commit gives no reason. The live model reads
#30 and #31 alike. A rule that makes it read #31 as guidance makes it read #30
as guidance too (candidate A below). Either #31 should expect `false`, or
#29 and #30 should expect `true`. That is a product call on whether a result
against a guided range should fetch the guidance.

## #57: the expectation is a pin

"Why did AMD's operating margin turn negative in Q2 2025?" expects route
`trend` because #504 pinned `margin_trend` when samples split three ways
(#511). Today the model reads it as `derived_margin` 30 of 30 times. A rule
that moves it (candidate B) moves #49 too. So the pin decides #57's replay,
not the model.

## Candidates

A, after the "result set against expectations" rule:

```
- An outlook is guidance: a result set against the outlook's range ("within its outlook", "above the top of the
  outlook") asks guidance as well as explains.
```

B, after the operating and net margin rule:

```
- A margin that turned negative or positive, or flipped to a loss, in a quarter or year changed against the one
  before: "why did gross margin turn positive in Q3 2024?" asks margins and margin_trend.
```
